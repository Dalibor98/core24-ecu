01 / Anatomy

One box runs the whole engine.

The ECU decides how much fuel goes in, when, and at what pressure. Hundreds of times a second it reads boost, rail pressure, temperatures and load, then sets injection, turbo and exhaust treatment to match.

  • ADie-cast housing and heat sink
  • B32-bit main processor
  • CFlash memory, where the map lives
  • DInjector driver stages
  • EHarness connector to the truck

02 / Diagnose

We find the fault before it strands you.

We connect over the J1939 CAN bus and read every module: stored and pending fault codes, freeze-frame data and live sensor values. You get a clear report, not a guess.
